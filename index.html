01 / NOVA

A NEW LAYER FOR THE PHONE

One physical surface.
A connected experience.

NOVA brings selected functions into one companion system around the smartphone. It is built to work with the phone and its app, rather than compete with the phone itself.

Designed for the moments when the screen is not the best interface.

03 / THE SYSTEM

NOVA → PHONE → APP → AI

A case that belongs to an ecosystem.

NOVA is designed as the physical layer. The smartphone carries the primary computing capability, while the companion app connects controls, preferences and future AI experiences.

  • 01 Smartphone-led intelligence
  • 02 App-based personalization
  • 03 A foundation for future functions
04 / POWER

DESIGNED AS A COMPANION SYSTEM

Power that stays part of the phone.

NOVA is conceived as a case, not a separate power bank. When connected to external power, the intended system charges NOVA and the phone together.
